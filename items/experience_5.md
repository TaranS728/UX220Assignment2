## Learning to Drive

### What did I do?
I learned to drive through lessons with a driving instructor and practice with both my dad and a friend. Learning from different people gave me opportunities to practice and develop my driving skills.

### When did I do it?
I learned to drive from August 2016 to October 2017, just over a year.

### What was good about it?
Learning to drive gave me more independence. Being able to drive myself meant I didn't have to rely on other people to get around. 

### What was my favourite memory?
My favourite memory was my first solo drive after getting my G2 licence. It felt freeing to drive on my own after all the time I had spent learning with other people.
### What didn't I enjoy?
I did not enjoy the close calls when I almost got into accidents or when my instructor became upset with me. Those moments made the learning process very stressful.

### What grade would I give this experience?
**I would give it a C — It was okay.** Gaining independence was rewarding, but the stressful moments made the learning experience less enjoyable. I am just glad to be over with it.
