## Maintaining a Serious Gym Routine

### What did I do?
I followed a structured gym routine, training each muscle group 1 to 2 times per week. I averaged five days a week at the gym with the goal of losing fat and building muscle.

### When did I do it?
I started in May 2022, and I am still maintaining my routine to this day.

### What was good about it?
I enjoyed seeing my progress gradually over time. I became stronger, felt better about how I looked, and learned a lot throughout the process.

### What was my favourite memory?
My favourite memory was finally bench pressing one plate on each side. It was a big milestone because it provided me proof that I was getting stronger in the gym. 

### What didn't I enjoy?
I did not enjoy occasionally straining my neck and shoulders. These strains were very painful and I wouldn't be able to turn my neck for a few days.

### What grade would I give this experience?
**I would give it an A — Couldn't be better.** Despite the occasional strains, seeing my progress and learning throughout the process made working out very rewarding.
