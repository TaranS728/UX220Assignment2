## Working at Apple

### What did I do?
I sold Apple devices to the public and trained new hires.

### When did I do it?
I started in October 2023, and I am still working there currently.

### What was good about it?
This experience taught me a lot about public speaking and multitasking. Selling devices and training new hires gave me opportunities to practice communicating with different people and managing multiple responsibilities.

### What was my favourite memory?
My favourite memory was when a new hire I trained gave me a handwritten card thanking me for my patience with her during training. It was nice to know that she appreciated the support I provided.

### What didn't I enjoy?
I did not enjoy how busy and disorganized it became sometimes. This made it challenging to manage everything happening at once.

### What grade would I give this experience?
**I would give it a B — For the most part enjoyable.** I developed useful skills and had memorable experiences, although the busy and disorganized moments were challenging.
