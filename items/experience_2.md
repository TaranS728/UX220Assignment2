## Studying UX Design at Wilfrid Laurier University

### What did I do?
I studied User Experience Design at Wilfrid Laurier University.

### When did I do it?
I started in September 2020 and am continuing today, with a few gap years in between when I explored other things.

### What was good about it?
I learned a lot about UX design and how extensive the field really is. The program helped me explore different aspects of UX.

### What was my favourite memory?
My favourite memory was learning how to use Figma. I found it very fun to use because it gave me a way to be creative in my designing.

### What didn't I enjoy?
I did not enjoy the user testing aspect as much as the creative work. I prefer designing and modelling because those activities give me more freedom and creativity.

### What grade would I give this experience?
** I would give it a C — It was okay.** I enjoyed learning about UX and using creative tools like Figma, but some parts of the program didn't interest me very much.
