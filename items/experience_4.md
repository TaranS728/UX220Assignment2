## Learning to Troubleshoot Apple Devices

### What did I do?
I learned to troubleshoot a wide range of technical and software problems on Apple devices through trial and error. I developed the skills to help customers, friends, and family get their iPhones, MacBooks, iPads, and Apple Watches up and running again.

### When did I do it?
I started in October 2023 when I began working at Apple, and I have continued learning more since then.

### What was good about it?
I enjoyed gaining a better understanding of the technology over time and using that knowledge to help people solve their problems. As I learned more, I became more confident in my troubleshooting skills.

### What was my favourite memory?
My favourite memory was helping an older woman recover photos she had accidentally deleted. My knowledge of iOS helped me find them in the Recently Deleted section of the Photos app and restore them for her. It was nice to use what I had learned to help someone solve a problem.

### What didn't I enjoy?
I did not enjoy making mistakes while learning, especially when I accidentally made a customer's situation worse. Those moments were frustrating and scary, but I learned from these errors and used them to improve my troubleshooting skills.

### What grade would I give this experience?
**I would give it a B — For the most part enjoyable.** Helping people and understanding technology better was rewarding, even though learning through mistakes could be frustrating.
